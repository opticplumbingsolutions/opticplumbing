Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information Optic Plumbing Solutions collects, why we collect it, and how we look after it. We only collect what we need to quote, book and do your plumbing work.

Who we are

Optic Plumbing Solutions (ABN 28 654 678 449) is a licensed NSW plumbing business (licence 368355C) based in Concord, NSW. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Optic Plumbing Solutions.

What we collect

  • When you call, text or email us: your name, phone number, email address, the job address, details of the plumbing problem, and any photos or plans you send.
  • When you use the quote form on this website: your name, phone number, the type of job, how urgent it is, and your message.
  • When we do a job: site and access details, the work done, quotes, invoices and payment records.
  • When you visit this website: we don't use analytics or advertising cookies. Like any website, the companies that host and deliver this site see basic technical details, such as your IP address and browser type, when the page loads.

How we use it

  • To answer your enquiry, give you a quote and book the job.
  • To do the work, keep you updated, invoice you and follow up.
  • To meet our legal obligations, such as plumbing compliance paperwork, tax and business records.

We never sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only who we need to, to get your job done:

  • Our own team and any subcontractor working on your job.
  • The service providers that run our business systems, such as our email, accounting software, website hosting and the service that delivers our website form (Formspree). They handle it on our behalf.
  • Government bodies or regulators when the law requires it.

Our business email and Google

We use a private tool, called Jarvis, that connects to our own business Gmail account through Google's Gmail API. Only Optic Plumbing Solutions uses it. It does not access anyone else's Google account.

It lets us read and search emails sent to our business, such as quote requests, plans from builders and payment questions, and prepare draft replies. A person at Optic Plumbing Solutions checks every email before it is sent.

Information from Gmail is only used to run our business and reply to our customers. We don't sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers that run the tool for us.

How we keep it safe

Your information is kept in our email, our accounting software and on our business computer, all protected by passwords. We keep it for as long as we need it for your job and for as long as the law requires us to keep business and tax records.

Your choices

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we're not required to keep it. Just contact us using the details below.

If you have a privacy complaint, please contact us first so we can fix it. If you're not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
