— Know the signs

Signs your drain
is blocking up

Catch a blockage early and it's a quick clear. Leave it and you risk sewage backing up into the house. If you spot these, get it looked at.

Slow-draining water

Sinks, showers or basins emptying slower than usual — the first warning a blockage is forming.

Gurgling sounds

A toilet or drain that gurgles when another fixture runs means trapped air behind a partial blockage.

Bad smells

A rotten or sewage smell from a drain points to trapped waste — common with sewer-line blockages.

Water backing up

Water rising in the shower when the toilet flushes is a classic sign of a main-line blockage.

Multiple fixtures affected

If several drains block at once, the problem is in the shared sewer line, not one fixture. Call now.

Sewage overflow

Sewage coming up through a floor waste or gully is an emergency — health hazard and water damage. Call 24/7.

— Why it happens here

Concord's older homes,
older pipes

Much of Concord, Cabarita and Mortlake was built when sewer lines were laid in clay and earthenware pipe. Decades on, those joints crack and shift — and the mature trees the area is known for send roots straight into them, chasing water.

That's why so many Concord blockages come back: a plunger or a quick snake clears the symptom, but the root mass and the cracked pipe are still there. The only way to fix it for good is to see inside the pipe, cut the roots out under high pressure, and reline the damaged section.

Common questions

How much does it cost to clear a blocked drain in Sydney?
A straightforward blocked sink, basin or single toilet in Concord is typically cleared from $150–$350. A deeper sewer or stormwater blockage that needs a CCTV camera to locate it and high-pressure jet blasting to clear it usually falls in the $350–$700 range. We charge no callout fee on booked jobs and give you a fixed price on-site before any work starts.
Why does my drain keep blocking?
Because the cause hasn't been removed — only the symptom. In Concord's older homes the usual culprit is tree-root intrusion into aging clay sewer pipes, plus fat, wipes and debris build-up. A CCTV inspection finds the exact cause so it can be cleared with jet blasting and, if the pipe is cracked, repaired with relining — so it stops coming back.
Is a blocked drain an emergency?
It is when sewage is backing up into the house, several fixtures stop draining at once, or wastewater can't drain at all — these risk health hazards and water damage. Optic Plumbing Solutions attends blocked-drain emergencies in Concord 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
Can I clear a blocked sewer myself?
You can often clear a minor sink blockage with a plunger or hot water. A blocked sewer is different — chemical drain cleaners rarely shift root masses and can damage pipes, and a hand snake won't fix a collapsed pipe. If the toilet gurgles, multiple fixtures back up, or the blockage returns, it needs a licensed drainer with jet blasting and CCTV.
